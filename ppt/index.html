01 · Background

From a TV scene to a research question

The Chinese TV drama A Little Reunion made the pressure visibleKnown in Chinese as Xiao Huan Xi / 小欢喜, the story follows families during a child's high-stakes senior year.

One parent carries several roles at onceWork expectations, child care, elder care, and emotional labor collide in one day.

That scene led me to ask a larger questionLiterature review later confirmed that this time poverty also exists beyond fiction.

02 · Research Question
Research Question

How does an interactive task-list demo affect users' awareness of sandwich generation time poverty and its impact on family members?

Hypothesis

After using the demo, users will better understand the issue as serious, common, and structural.

Why It Matters

The issue is often mistaken for poor personal time management. This project frames it as overlapping work, child-care, elder-care, and family responsibilities competing for the same limited time.

03 · Demo Design

Interactive Game Design

Drag the parent to glowing task locationsWork, child care, elder care, hospital visits, and self-time compete for the same day.

The task list acts like pressureIt looks like choice, but most tasks are socially necessary.

07 · Discussion

Discussion

The interactive website helped increase awareness of sandwich-generation time poverty.
Limitation
  • The sample is still small.
  • The current analysis lacks deeper qualitative evidence.
Future expectation
  • Continue increasing sample size through RedNote, TikTok, and other social media.
  • Invite completed participants for in-depth interviews.
